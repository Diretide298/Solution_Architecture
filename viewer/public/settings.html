What your role can do
Name and address
Set when your invitation was accepted. The address is what you sign in with.

Password

At least 12 characters. Changing it signs out every other session on your account — which is the point, if you think somebody else has it. If you use the Claude connector, avoid quote characters, and update the connector afterwards.

OpenProject not connected

Your own token, not a shared one. Anything ADAM does in the PMS is done as you, so the history there names a person rather than a robot. It is encrypted before it is stored and never sent back to this page — you will see the last four characters, which is enough to recognise which one you pasted.

Make one in OpenProject under My account → Access tokens. ADAM checks it against the instance before keeping it — a token that does not work is worse than none, because it looks configured and fails later somewhere that reads as a different fault. Removing it here does not revoke it there.

Git identity

The address your commits carry, when it differs from the one you sign in with. It is what lets a commit be matched back to the work package it belongs to. Not a secret, and blank is fine — blank honestly means "not stated" rather than a guess nobody made.

Claude connector

Twenty-two tools that let Claude Code on your machine read this package — screens, journeys, contracts, tables, services, modules and the decisions behind them — and, once OpenProject is connected above, work your tickets: pull one with everything it touches into .adam/ in your folder, then propose its new status and apply it once you have said yes.

The easy way: ask an ADAM admin for the connector setup zip, unzip it and double-click setup.cmd. It checks your password, installs the connector and registers it with Claude Code — nothing below is needed.

Or by hand, once, in PowerShell or Git Bash — with Node.js 22+ and a copy of viewer/mcp

…

Put your own password in place of YOUR_PASSWORD and the path to your copy in place of PATH-TO-REPO. Keep every -e before the '--', and keep the quotes on '--' — PowerShell drops a bare one. Not in Command Prompt. Then check it with claude mcp get adam: all three values should be listed under Environment.

Everything it can read is what you can read — it signs in as your account and inherits the same refusals. There is no shared login, deliberately: one would hand every reader the widest role in the building.

Agent activity

Claude Code runs on your machine, so ADAM only knows what its hooks send: which tools ran, whether they worked, how long they took, and which ticket was open. Never the prompts, the arguments or the code. That is the whole list, and the service drops anything else it is sent.

Agents: where the time goes and what keeps failing Your own sessions; an admin or a project manager sees the team’s

Installed per machine, alongside the connector — see viewer/mcp/hooks/README.md. A session is sent when it ends, not while it runs, so nothing here slows your work down. Remove the hooks and everything else carries on exactly the same.

Sessions

Sign out ends the session in this browser. Sign out everywhere is for the sessions you cannot reach — a laptop left somewhere, a borrowed machine. It ends every session on your account, this browser included. Nothing else changes: your account stays enabled, and every verdict you have recorded keeps your name on it.

End every session on your account? You will be signed out here too, and sent back to the sign-in page.
