Accounts

An account is disabled rather than deleted, because a verdict points at the person who gave it. Deleting would either orphan the verdict or take it with them, and both lose something worth keeping.

End sessions signs somebody out of every browser and stops there. It is not Disable: the account still works, they can sign back in, and nothing they have recorded moves. It is the answer to a lost laptop or a shared machine, not to somebody leaving.
