Invite someone

The address is fixed here, by you, and cannot be changed by whoever opens the link. That is what makes an invite count as having verified it — so send the link however you already talk to them. It works once, and only for that address.

A client is outside the company. They read everything except Decisions — the ADRs, the registers and the conflict log. They can record a verdict, kept as the client review rather than the team's, and they cannot close an item. The @softlabsgroup.com rule does not apply, so the address can be anywhere; you typing it is what vouches for it. The link expires in three days whatever is chosen above.

Accounts

An account is disabled rather than deleted, because a verdict points at the person who gave it. Deleting would either orphan the verdict or take it with them, and both lose something worth keeping.

End sessions signs somebody out of every browser and stops there. It is not Disable: the account still works, they can sign back in, and nothing they have recorded moves. It is the answer to a lost laptop or a shared machine, not to somebody leaving.

Download a date range

A month of the register, as a CSV. The reviews page exports the rows it is showing you; this one takes a span of dates whether or not anybody has it on screen, which is what a report at the end of a month is made of. Same columns, same wording, so the two files stack.

Both dates are included, whole. A row recorded at any time on the To day is in the file. Leave From empty for everything from the beginning, or To empty for everything up to now — the filename says which range you took either way. Dates are read in UTC, which is the only clock anything here is stamped with.

Close items from a spreadsheet

The other end of the download above. Take the range, fill in the our verdict column against the items that have been dealt with — Built, Wired, Answered, Accepted or Approved — no action — and upload it here. The CSV as it came, or the same sheet saved as .xlsx; what the file is read as comes from what is inside it, not from what it is called.

Nothing is written until the second press. Checking the file reports what it would do — what closes, what is already closed, what disagrees with the register and what could not be read — and closes nothing. A blank cell is no decision, not a close, so a sheet full of empty cells does nothing at all. Items already closed are left alone: closing them again would replace who closed them and when with you and today. Rows are matched by the id column, so a file exported before that column existed cannot be applied — take the range again.
