Throughput

States

Every status OpenProject has for this project's tickets, in its own order. ADAM keeps no list of its own — a team that renames a column keeps its numbers.

People

What each person is holding: open tickets, and how many of those are on hold or past their due date. Finished work is counted apart, so a long-serving account does not read as overloaded.

Overdue and due

Open tickets whose due date has passed, then what lands in the next seven days. A ticket with no due date cannot appear in either, which is worth knowing about a board that looks empty here.

Overdue

Due in the next seven days

Timeline

Every epic that has dates, under the milestone it is being delivered for. A bar runs from the earliest start to the latest date among its tickets; the solid part is how much of it is closed. Tickets with no dates cannot be placed and are counted underneath rather than quietly dropped.

Deliverables

The parts of the package that tickets are linked to — a screen, a contract operation, a table — each with the tickets working on it. This is the join only ADAM can make: OpenProject knows the ticket, the package knows the artefact, and a link says they are the same piece of work.

Epics

Tickets grouped under their parent in OpenProject, with how far each group has got. Progress counts tickets finished, not percentages typed on them.
