    Features by sprint

    Every feature under its block, drawn across the sprints its tickets are in, or their own dates where a ticket has them. The solid part of a bar is how much of the feature is finished. Tickets with neither a sprint nor a date cannot be placed and are counted underneath rather than quietly dropped.

    Deliverables

    The parts of the package that tickets are linked to — a screen, a contract operation, a table — each with the tickets working on it. This is the join only ADAM can make: OpenProject knows the ticket, the package knows the artefact, and a link says they are the same piece of work.

    By parent

    Tickets grouped under their parent in OpenProject, biggest first, with how far each group has got. Progress counts tickets finished, not percentages typed on them.
