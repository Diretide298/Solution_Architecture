Ask about the package

Answers come from the package and nothing else. ADAM searches it for what you asked about, sends those excerpts with your question, and is told to say which part of the package would have the answer rather than inventing one. If the package looks wrong, you can raise a change request straight from the answer.

This provider needs a key before it can answer. ADAM asks on your own key, never on a shared one, so your questions are on your bill and under your rate limit and nobody else’s. It is encrypted before it is stored and never sent back to this page.
