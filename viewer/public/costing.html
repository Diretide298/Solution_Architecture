What it has cost

Hours come from the tickets; rates are set here; the cost is a multiplication done when you open the page. No total is stored, so nothing here can go stale — and nothing here is an invoice. The hours are the time people logged in OpenProject, which is not the same as the time they worked.

Per person

Logged hours against the rate set for that name. Somebody with hours and no rate shows a dash rather than a zero — a zero reads as free.

Hourly rates

Set against the name OpenProject uses, because that is what the hours arrive attached to. A rate typed against a name the tickets do not use matches nothing, so pick from the list.

Rates are set by an administrator. You can read them.

What is missing

Counted rather than absorbed, because each of these makes the total above an understatement and they are three different problems.
