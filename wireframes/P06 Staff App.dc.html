Dark, because of where it is used

Daylight at a gate, one hand, sometimes gloves. Targets stay large, the outcome fills the screen and the reason is always on it — the same rules as the P07 handheld, because it is often the same person.

CF-41 is a tab in the shell

The AI tab is in the delivered reference, which means the shell cannot ship without deciding what sits behind it. AI-61→66 is a Wave 1 commitment of roughly four weeks and there is no AI engineer on the project.

It shares the scanner’s contract

The scan group is validateAccess, overrideAccess and syncScans — the same operations as P07. This is not a second scanner; it is the scanner for staff who are not standing at a gate.

GOES TOEMP-017
Handed on
The shell was the easy part

Home · Tasks · Scan · AI · More is settled by the client reference. What is behind Tasks and AI is not settled by anything, and those two tabs hold most of the unknowns on this board.

Shared, not duplicated

The scan loop, the override, the offline journal and the reconciliation item are P07’s components. Build them once and this app is a different arrangement of the same parts.

Three screens nobody asked for

Sign-out refuses while a journal is unsynced, the journal can be handed to another device before a battery dies, and gate modes get put back after an evacuation. All three exist because the contract makes the failure possible and nothing else catches it.
