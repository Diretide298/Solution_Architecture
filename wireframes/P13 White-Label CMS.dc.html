Platform 13 · Tenant CMS

White-Label CMS

Twenty screens inventoried and none defined — but this is the least original of the boards, and deliberately so. The client has already designed the white-label builder across six reference boards. What was missing was everything around it: translations, publishing, versioning, legal notices and who in the tenant may press publish.

The builder already exists

Six delivered boards settle the live-preview device frame, the Publishing Status stepper and the brand controls. This board reuses them and fills in what surrounds them.

One token set, three themes

Light guest, dark staff, light POS. The palette on CMS-002 renders all three. The alternative — three independent systems — costs three times the maintenance and guarantees a status pill drifts.

CF-42 is not optional

The preview must toggle platform, theme and direction. RTL is a layout, and a tenant who cannot see their Arabic site before publishing it will publish a broken one.

A tenant sets the brand once, edits content weekly, and publishes with an approval behind it.

GOES TOCMS-016
Handed on
Reuse the six boards

Nothing on CMS-002 to CMS-006 should be redesigned. They exist, they are coherent, and the job is extracting them into named components against one token set.

What was actually missing

Translations, RTL preview, publishing workflow, version history, consent notice versions and tenant user access. None of those appear in the reference, and every one of them blocks a real publish.

One record, two screens

CMS-017 and ADM-017 are the same domain object seen from the tenant side and the platform side. A tenant cannot change what the platform has locked, and the screen has to say which is which.
