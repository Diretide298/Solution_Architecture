Platform 02 · Guest Mobile App

Guest App

Sixty-two screens inventoried and every one of them arrived with a placeholder purpose line. The purposes below are written from the contracts, the states are the ones the contracts declare, and the layouts differ because the jobs do — a QR at a turnstile, a wait-time heatmap, a kitchen tracker and a lease countdown are not the same screen in four coats of paint.

It is two apps in one shell

Before the visit it is a storefront; inside the venue it is a companion — map, queue, wait times, food, notifications. GST-001 changes shape between the two, and nothing else does.

The QR is the whole product

GST-012, GST-013 and GST-055 exist so a guest can show a code in a queue. A rotating code defeats screenshots and defeats offline guests at the same time, and that decision changes what the scanner has to cache.

RTL is a layout, not a translation

GST-043 mirrors the entire app. CF-42 requires the same of the white-label preview, and 38 localisation requirements sit behind both with no key inventory.

Flow map

Five tabs. The purchase path runs across Explore; everything in Plan only makes sense once the guest is on site.
