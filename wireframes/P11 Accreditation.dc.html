Accreditation Portal

Eight screens, the smallest board here, and the one most likely to be designed twice. Accreditation is workshop-blocked — 58 requirements with no contract behind them — while a Wave 1 scanner is expected to read the credential this portal issues.

Design last, or design twice

Every field below is inferred from the requirement register, not read off a contract. When the workshop lands, expect the document set and the zone model to move.

One shared component

ACC-002 is WEB-011 with an extension slot, and the consent block is the same everywhere. Honouring a consent given against a superseded notice is the compliance failure.

It blocks the scanner

ACC-005 issues the credential P07 reads. Until this contract exists, a Wave 1 scanner pointed at a staff pass has nothing behind it.
