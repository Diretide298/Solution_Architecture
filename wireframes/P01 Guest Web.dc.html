Platform 01 · Storefront

Guest Web

Twenty-nine screens, and the only surface with a working demo to converge on. It is a shopfront before it is software: photography, one clear next step per screen, and a lease countdown from the moment a session is chosen. F01 and F02 both run end to end through it.

The purchase path is nine screens

001 → 004 → 005 → 006 → 010 → 011 → 012 → 013 → 018. Everything else is entered from it or recovers from it. Design that spine and the rest is variation.

Thirteen of twenty-nine are stubs

Account, membership, loyalty, help and the error page are named, counted, and nobody has said what is on them. Fields below are inferred from the contracts and marked.

A clock runs from WEB-006

The lease countdown appears on four screens and the consent block on three more. Both are shared components, and both are compliance-relevant when they drift.

GOES TOWEB-006WEB-002
Handed on
Converge on the demo, do not restart

The Lovable prototype already settles the browse language — place-first navigation, editorial cards, one clear next step. This board keeps that and adds the states a prototype never has to show.

Five components carry it

Payment, the lease countdown, the consent block, the identity field group and the status pill. Four of the five also appear on POS, Partner and the guest app.

WEB-029 should be four screens

Sold out, payment failed, maintenance and not found have different recoveries. Sold-out keeps the basket; maintenance cannot. One page cannot serve all four.
