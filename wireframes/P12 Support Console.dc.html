Support Console

Eight screens, the smallest surface on the estate, and the place every other platform’s failure arrives: a denied scan, a payment stuck in unknown, a refund that consumed a single-use code. It is the last place a bad day can be fixed.

It reads four contracts at once

SUP-005 needs the case, the order, the entitlement and the refund path in one view — and not one of those read paths may admit anybody. lookupTicket exists for exactly this.

Two of eight are configuration

SUP-003 and SUP-007 are the scoped settings template used twelve times on Admin. Build it once and these two stop being design work.

SLA targets do not exist

30 performance requirements carry one number between them, so SUP-008 is drawn against targets nobody has agreed.

Handed on
The console is a reader, not an owner

It owns cases and canned responses. Orders, tickets, payments and refunds all belong to other contracts and are read across, under permissions that cannot admit anybody.

Two of eight are not support screens

SUP-003 and SUP-007 are the scoped settings template. That is configuration, not design.

Blocked

No SLA targets, no notification catalogue and no audit register — three of the fifteen artefact classes that touch this console are still open.
