Guest Kiosk

Not inventoried, not defined, and white-labelled — so this board starts by asking what a kiosk is for. The answer narrows it hard: a guest standing in a corridor, avoiding a queue, with a card and about ninety seconds of patience. Fourteen screens, card only, and two of them are failures.

Card only, and that is a design decision

A kiosk has no drawer, no blind count and nobody accountable for a variance. Cash here would put an unreconciled float in a public corridor, so the shift controls that make POS safe simply do not exist.

Nobody is standing next to it

On every other surface a person resolves a payment stuck in unknown. Here nobody does, so KSK-008 has to call staff rather than time out — and that behaviour is unspecified everywhere in the package.

White-labelled, so it reads the CMS

The attract loop, the palette and the language list all come from the tenant brand kit on P13. A kiosk is the most public surface a tenant has and the least supervised.

It had to be inventoried before it could be designed

The kiosk was counted at zero. These fourteen are a proposal for the inventory, derived from the same order, payment and access contracts the other surfaces use.

Nothing here is new

Products, availability, leases, payment and media issuance all exist. The kiosk is a different arrangement of them with much larger targets and no operator.

Two open questions

What a kiosk does when a payment stays unknown and nobody is watching, and whether an unattended device may print media for a booking it cannot verify the holder of.
