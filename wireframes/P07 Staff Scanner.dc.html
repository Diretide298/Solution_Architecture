Staff Scanner

Sixteen screens, none of which existed before this board. The scanner was inventoried at zero and defined at zero, and it is a Wave 1 surface — so the screens below are derived operation by operation from contracts/spine/access.yaml rather than from a screen list, and every card cites the operation it came from.

The premise

Dark theme, not a style choice. This is a handheld held at a gate in daylight and at night, often one-handed, sometimes gloved. Targets are 56px and up, the outcome fills the screen, and the reason is always on it.

Offline is the default assumption

The contract says validation must work with no network. Every screen therefore has an offline form, and the reconciliation screen — where the server downgrades a local admit to a denial — is designed, not deferred.

Still unresolved

Turnstile hardware control is out of contract and the vendor SDK is outstanding, so SCN-016 shows gate mode as a request, not a confirmed hardware state. Accreditation credentials are workshop-blocked and are not scanned here yet.

Handed on
Sixteen screens, now specified

The scanner went from an uninventoried Wave 1 surface to a named screen set with field names taken from the contract. These IDs can go straight into the screen YAML.

Two components are shared

The outcome card (SCN-004/005/010/011) is one component with four states, and the offline journal is the same component the POS uses for its own queue. Neither should be built twice.

What still blocks

Accreditation credentials are workshop-blocked, so a scanner reading a staff pass has no contract behind it. Turnstile hardware is a driver concern with no vendor SDK.
