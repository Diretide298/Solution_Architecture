Platform 08 · Venue Back Office

Staff Web — Back Office

Seventy-three screens inventoried and six defined. The other 67 below are derived operation by operation from the contracts, because a back office is not a feature list — it is whatever the venue has to do when the automatic path fails, and every one of those recoveries is already a contract operation.

This is the recovery surface

A feed dies, a till does not balance, a card charges with no response, a handheld admits somebody offline that the server would have denied. Every one of those lands on a screen here, and none of them were among the six.

It owns what the gate enforces

Admission profiles, blacklist and closures are edited here and enforced on the handheld — through the offline package, so a change at 14:00 takes effect at the next refresh rather than at 14:00.

Three screens catch other platforms

The reconciliation queue receives P07’s divergences, payment exceptions resolves what POS-005 could not, and capacity is the other side of the partner allocation on PTR-005.

Flow map

Twelve modules. A duty manager lives in Queue, Access and Cash; everything else is set up once and visited when something breaks.
