Staff POS

Ten screens, six of which existed as client mockups and four of which did not. The four that did not are the ones that decide whether the day balances: held orders, the blind close, the roster, and appending onto media a guest already holds.

Highest revenue risk

Every sale at the gate runs through these ten screens, and the offline behaviour is invisible in a static frame. Cash completes locally and journals; card does not.

The blind count is the control

POS-007 withholds the expected figure until submission. It is POS-001’s denomination grid with one flag flipped — one component, not two screens.

Handed on
Two components, four screens

The denomination counter is POS-001 and POS-007 with a blind flag. The payment panel is shared with WEB-012, GST-009 and PTR-012 — one design, four platforms, two offline contracts.

What the mockups missed

Held Orders, the blind close, the roster and Add to Existing Ticket. A Hold button with no way to find the hold is a lost sale.

Offline contract

Cash completes locally and journals. Card does not — an offline card approval the acquirer never saw is a sale that vanishes at settlement.
