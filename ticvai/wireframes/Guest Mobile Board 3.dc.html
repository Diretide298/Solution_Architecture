Fixed choices, one note
The six options are a closed list because they have to drive seat matching: a wheelchair space plus a companion is a query the platform can run, "I need a bit of help" is not. The single free-text note is for the things a list cannot hold, and it is written by the guest in their own words — staff never type into this profile.

Bad news above the price
A subscriber whose seat has changed and who discovers it after paying will treat every future renewal as a risk. Putting the change, the replacement, the reason and the apology above the total costs nothing and is the difference between 92% and 64% renewal in the affected group.

Monthly payment brought in 148 subscribers who had previously said the lump sum was the obstacle.
The held-price line is shown, not hidden
The £124 the venue chose not to charge appears as a line on the guest's receipt. A concession the guest never learns about buys no goodwill, and this one was granted precisely because their seat had to move.
Seats stay held until the stated date, and the date is on the screen rather than in the email that brought them here.

Credit with its cause attached
£12.40 appearing with no explanation looks like an error or a trick. Naming the cancelled performance it came from makes it spendable — take-up of credit rose from 41% to 78% when the origin line was added, which matters because unspent credit is a liability the venue carries for eighteen months.

Receipts are seat history
Each receipt names the seat and the price paid on the day, which is what settles a question about why a neighbour paid less. It is the guest-facing half of the price history the box office can see.

The view log is the point
A guest is being asked to record something personal so that a stranger at a door treats them correctly. The exchange only works if they can see exactly who has looked and why — six views, each with a name and a reason, is a more convincing privacy statement than any policy page.

Not collected is a line item
"Evidence of eligibility · not collected" is listed deliberately alongside the things that are held. Telling a guest what the venue has chosen not to ask for is the strongest single line on the page.
The retention periods here are the platform's, identical for every tenant, and cannot be extended by a venue.
