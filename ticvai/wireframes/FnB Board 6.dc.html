Why is food cost up 1.4 points this month?
Three causes account for 1.9 points of pressure, offset by 0.5 points of pricing and mix gains.

Supplier inflation is the largest at +0.9 pts, led by beef patty +11.2% and cocoa +18.4%. Waste adds +0.6 pts, concentrated in seafood trim at Main Restaurant and expiry at Beach Kiosk. Yield adds +0.4 pts, almost entirely the sea bass filleting batch running 8 points under recipe.
FNB-6E food cost bridge FNB-5C variance FNB-5D batch 115
What should we do about the sea bass?
Two options, and they are not exclusive. Re-spec the recipe yield to the observed 60% and raise the dish 4.00 to hold margin, which recovers about 9k a quarter. Or run a yield test across two shifts first — if technique is the cause, retraining is cheaper than a price rise.
