A policy rejection is usually about our sending reputation rather than the address. The complaint rate being over threshold is the likely cause, which makes this a symptom of the newsletter.
Retry rules
A hard bounce is never retried and the address is suppressed immediately. A soft failure is retried with widening gaps. A transactional message that exhausts its retries creates a task; a marketing one is simply dropped.
