Personalisation, all three rules
Show the venue they last visited firston the multi-venue landing page only
Keep access preferences prefilledso a wheelchair user does not re-declare it every booking
Remember the language they choseacross sites and devices
All three remove a step the guest has already taken. None of them changes what is offered or what it costs.
Rejected, with reasons
×Reorder the listing by predicted taste · hides events people would have chosen
×Show a higher price band first to high spenders · different prices for the same seat
×"Because you liked…" on event pages · reveals inference the guest never shared
×Hide sold-out dates from returning members · the absence is itself confusing
The second one was proposed twice. It is price discrimination with a friendly name, and it is recorded here so it does not arrive a third time.
Remove steps, do not reshape the offer
Personalisation that saves a guest typing is welcome. Personalisation that decides what they are shown or what they pay is a different thing wearing the same word.
