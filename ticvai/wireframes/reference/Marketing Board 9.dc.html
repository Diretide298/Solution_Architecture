Twenty-one guests raised their public rating after a reply, without being asked to. None of them were offered anything; they were answered by a person who had read the booking.
What is never automated
×The apology text
×The size of a refund or voucher
×Public replies to reviews
×Closing a case the guest has not answered
An automated apology is worse than silence: the guest can tell, and it removes the one thing they asked for, which is a person's attention.
What the guest receives instantly
"Thank you for telling us. Ravi from our team has your note and will write to you himself by 15:00 today."
It names a person and a time, which is the only promise an automated message is allowed to make.
Automate the routing, not the relationship
The machinery gets the right case to the right desk with a deadline. Everything the guest actually reads is written by the person whose name is on it.
