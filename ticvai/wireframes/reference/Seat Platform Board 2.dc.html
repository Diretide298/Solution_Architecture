Board 1 drew the room. This board fills it with seats that know what they are. A seat number is a label a guest reads aloud to an usher, so it has to survive renumbering, row insertions and a house that counts from the aisle in one hall and from the wall in another. Everything else here — sightline, step count, width, legroom, obstruction — is a physical fact recorded once by someone standing in the seat, and reused by every price, every listing and every refund argument afterwards.
