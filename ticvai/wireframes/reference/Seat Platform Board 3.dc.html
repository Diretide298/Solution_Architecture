Access seating fails in a predictable way: the spaces exist, but they are held off sale "just in case", released too late, or sold without the companion seat beside them. This board treats access as ordinary inventory with extra facts attached — a wheelchair space is a space with a companion seat, a transfer route and a sightline, bookable online at the same moment as every other seat, and never released by an email to one person who is on holiday.
