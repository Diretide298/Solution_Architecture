A price band is a promise about what a guest will see and how easily they will reach it. When bands are drawn by eye, guests in the same band get different experiences and the house pays for it in refunds. Here the bands come from the seat attributes captured on Board 2, prices sit in a grid that can be read across a whole run, and every change is compared against what the seats actually sold for last time rather than against a target somebody typed in March.

Shapes, not rows
Demand in a proscenium house is a cone from the centre of the stage, not a set of horizontal stripes. Bands drawn as stripes overprice edges and underprice the middle.
