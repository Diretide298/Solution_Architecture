A box office sale is a conversation with a keyboard attached. The person on the counter knows things the website cannot: that a guest hates stairs, that a party of six will not split, that the man on the phone is buying for his mother's birthday. These screens are built for someone typing while talking — keyboard first, seat facts in reach, and every discount and override recorded with a reason rather than argued about later.
