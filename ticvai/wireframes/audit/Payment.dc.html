UNCHANGED IN THE 1 SEP DUMP

The component library is unambiguous about this one: unknown is “the important one. A terminal that charged a card and never returned a response is the normal failure, not the exotic one — the screen must offer inquiry, not a retry that double-charges.” POS-005 declares inquirePaymentStatus and then offers the retry.

Four changes. unknown becomes a declared state and reaches for the operation the screen already lists. Retry is locked until the inquiry answers. Cancel order is disabled while money is in flight — you cannot void what you cannot confirm. The two receipt buttons move to the post-sale screen, taking the bar from six to four.
