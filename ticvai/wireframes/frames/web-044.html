We arrive Friday with a four-year-old. Which slides can she go on?
Four-year-olds can use the Kids’ Lagoon, the two family rafts on the lower tower and the lazy river with an adult. The tower slides need 1.2 m and the Flowrider needs 1.3 m, so those are out this visit.FAQ-SAFETY · height rulesFAQ-ENTRY
Is Friday very busy?
Friday is the busiest day of the week and the tower slides were at 45 minutes yesterday afternoon. Mornings before 11:00 are quieter, and the Kids’ Lagoon rarely queues.getWaitTimes · yesterdayvenue programme
