Accessibility at Marina Bay
Step-free access
Every pool, both restaurants and the arena are reachable without steps. Two of the four slide towers are not, and the map marks which.
→ WEB-039
Pool hoists
At the wave pool and the kids’ lagoon. Staffed, no notice needed, no charge.
2 locations
Carer tickets
A carer accompanying a disabled guest enters free. Bring the guest’s proof; the carer needs nothing.
free
Quiet hour
Fridays 08:00 to 09:00. Music off, wave machine off, half capacity. Booked as an ordinary day pass.
Fri 08:00
Changing Places
One full facility with a hoist and adult-sized bench, by Gate A. Radar key or ask any lifeguard.

Assistance dogs
Welcome on site, not in the water. Shaded rest area by the arena.
