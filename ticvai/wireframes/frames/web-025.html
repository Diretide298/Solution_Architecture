Tickets and entry14 answers
Can I enter and leave on the same ticket?Yes on a day pass, up to four entries. The QR is re-read on the way back in, and the entry count is on your ticket in My Tickets.
What if I arrive after my session starts?A timed session admits you up to 30 minutes late. After that the entry is refused at the gate and the ticket becomes a credit rather than a loss.
Do children need their own ticket?Under threes are free and do not need one. Everyone else does, including anyone accompanying a child and not swimming.
My QR will not scan.The code rotates every 30 seconds, so a screenshot will not work. Open the ticket in the app or the browser you booked in and show the live one.
Can someone else use my ticket?Only if you transfer it. A transferred ticket is reissued to them and yours stops scanning — sharing a screenshot does not work and gets both of you stopped.

If the answer is not here, raise it and a person picks it up. Guest Services answer inside four hours during opening, same day otherwise.
