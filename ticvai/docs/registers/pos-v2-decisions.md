# POS v2 decisions

> **Purpose:** what the POS v2 build does differently from the package, and what was decided  
> **Owner:** Chinmay  
> **Status:** Living. Generated by `tools/build-audit-decisions.py` from `handoff/pos-v2-decisions.json`

**Decided 1 October by Chinmay: all eight recommendations approved.** The POS v2 build
(`sources/designs/TICVAI_POS_Terminal_v2.html`, kept out of git) is our improved version of the
client-approved terminal and the client has not approved it; its captures are in
`wireframes/incoming/P04-pos-v2/`. The frames and their design corrections are applied by
`tools/applied/pos-v2-decisions-1-october.py`; the specification changes are in the screens and
contracts named below, and reach the tasks at the release refresh (r2).

**8 decisions.**

P04 had no screen for either. | One new P04 screen, POS-030 Sales Journal, for both. It lists the till's orders (by default this terminal, today) with reprint or email of the receipt, reprint of the order, the tax invoice, Refund or void handed to POS-011, and Reload this cart (getOrder, then addCartLine). listOrders gains optional terminal (workstationId), customer (subjectId) and payment-method (tender) query parameters, an additive change. | POSV2-2 | Reservations and group bookings at the till (left rail Bookings, F5 Reservations): a reservation without payment, deposits and balances, and a group's tickets encoded as Inactive before payment ('pay on arrival') and activated on payment (F6 Encode tickets, 'Set active', 'Activate all on payment'). | One new P04 screen, POS-031 Reservations & Group Arrivals, built on the existing operations only: createReservation, listReservations, getReservation, extendReservation, convertReservation and cancelReservation, and the group operations the back office uses (getGroupBooking as on BO-026; listGroupArrivalCheck and recordGroupCheckIn as on BO-280). ## Where v2 contradicts a decision (2)
