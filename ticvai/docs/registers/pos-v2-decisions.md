**Decided 1 October by Chinmay: all eight recommendations approved.** The POS v2 build
(`sources/designs/TICVAI_POS_Terminal_v2.html`, in git since 2 October: CHG-DOC-002) is our improved
version of the client-approved terminal and the client has not approved it; its captures are in
`wireframes/incoming/P04-pos-v2/`. The frames and their design corrections are applied by
`tools/applied/pos-v2-decisions-1-october.py`; the specification changes are in the screens and
contracts named below, and reach the tasks at the release refresh (r2).

(POSV2-9 to POSV2-13: POS-000, POS-003, POS-004, POS-012, POS-023; DEC-550 to DEC-554 in
[decisions-2-october](decisions-2-october.md)). A later decision that replaces part of a row is shown on it.
