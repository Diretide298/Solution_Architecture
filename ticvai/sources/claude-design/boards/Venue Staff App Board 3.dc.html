The last board, and the one furthest from the gate. Rota and attendance, the record a steward writes when something happens to somebody, the reference material behind the More tab, and the settings a shared handheld needs at the end of a shift. Same dark shell and 44px floor as Boards 1 and 2. Riverside Arts throughout.

Seventeen screens, and P06's generated set is drawn. Board 1 holds shift, scan and tasks (16); Board 2 gate ops, AI, comms and emergency (12), and this board the last 17. EMP-030 Venue map is on Seat-4C and the sixteen Floor Service screens are on FnB Boards 4 and 5, which is why they are absent here. 16 + 12 + 17 is 45, and the other 21 of P06's 66 are hosted by the client packs: sixteen Floor Service screens on FnB Boards 4 and 5, EMP-030 on Seat, and the four Stock on the Floor screens EMP-067–EMP-070 — FNB-5E, RET-4H, RET-4J and INV-10. 45 + 21 = 66, and P06 reconciles. Note that EMP-068 and EMP-070 are joined to those frames by title match alone — the packs label by board position, so the mapping is worth a human check.
This board carries the most bulk-attach residue of the three, and most of it has already been cut. EMP-043 Device settings had ten guest-CRM operations removed on 24 August — loyalty points, consent history, profile merging — and still requires the marketing module for a screen about screen brightness. Three rota screens had createRotaAssignment and updateRotaAssignment cut on the same day: a rota a steward can rewrite is not a rota. Still open: EMP-029 Guest assistance holds twelve operations across two contracts, and EMP-040 Knowledge base and EMP-041 Training are both defined against a single operation, semanticSearch — a training module is not a search box.
