P15 Kitchen Display — the pass and the stations (10 screens · 1 module)

kitchen-display · wall screen · touchLarge · offline-capable
1280 × 880 frames · board index

All ten screens of P15, drawn dark and large because of what the source says about them: bumped by somebody with flour on their hands, read from two metres, in a room where nobody is sitting down. Every screen is density: touchLarge and every one keeps working offline except the last. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant — the Bridge Kitchen, two stations and a pass.

These ten screens are drawn twice, on purpose. Each one carries a derivedFrom pointing at a frame in the client F&B pack — FnB Board 3, Kitchen & Orders, drawn 20 August and matched to these screens by frame title. That pack is the specification: it carries operations, states, entry params and exits, and it is better specified than anything derived from the screen file. What it is not is a kitchen display — the pack draws back-office frames at 1280×700 for someone sitting down. This board draws the same ten screens as the device they run on. The mapping is one to one: KIT-001–008 to FNB-3A–3H, then KIT-009 to 3J and KIT-010 to 3K.

Load is a promise-time question
A station is over capacity when the work on it cannot be finished by the promise times it holds — not when it has more tickets than another. That is why the percentage is drawn beside average prep time and the number of chefs, which are the two things that change it.

Chase is a message, not a status change

What this board found. Five, and four of them are the same shape: the operation the screen is named for exists in the contract and reaches no screen. rebalanceStationLoad on KIT-005 dynamic routing; sendOrderNotification on KIT-007 digital notification; setKitchenSla on KIT-009 SLA, where setVenueSettings is declared instead; and the three HACCP corrective-action operations on KIT-008, which reads food-safety status and cannot record what was done about it. All four sit in P15's own list of 31 operations with no screen — the gap table names them and nothing had put them beside the screen that wants them. Fifth: KIT-004 is called management and declares two reads, so either the name or the operations are wrong.
