Twenty screens, one module, all wave 2 — where a tenant sets its brand, builds its storefront and publishes. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant. This is the platform CF-77's bulk attach hit hardest: 41 identical operations were removed on 24 August, and what remains on some screens still belongs to another platform entirely. Each frame draws the screen its name promises and names the operations that do not fit.

The screen count disagrees with its source, again — and by more than P12 did. The derived platform page says 20 screens; screens/P13-white-label-cms.yaml holds 60, CMS-001 to CMS-060. The forty extras are two whole domains that arrived from client workshops — privacy, consent and preference management (CMS-021–040) and waiver and digital form management (CMS-041–060) — and every one of them points at a workshopBoard among WS33, WS34, WS58 and WS59, none of which exists in wireframes/. With P12's twenty that is sixty screens the platform pages do not count, across four platforms' worth of missing workshop boards. The twenty drawn here are the twenty the platform declares.

What this board found. Twenty screens, 77 declared operations, splitting three ways. Four are clean — Brand Kit, Theme, Media, Publishing — and Media is the best-specified screen on this platform, though two of its twelve come from orders rather than assets. Three carry operations from another domain — each confirmed against the contract field, not inferred from the name: CMS-004 declares the maintenance asset lifecycle for logos, CMS-009 declares F&B menu management for site navigation, and CMS-018 declares eight guest-record operations — including adjustLoyaltyPoints — on a legal-notices screen. CMS-001 is the largest: twenty-one platform-admin operations that create, suspend and terminate tenants and set subscriptions, on a venue-facing screen. Six screens cannot write what they exist to write — SEO, translations, blocks, site settings, user access, domains — and two of the missing writes, setSeoMetadata and createUrlRedirect, sit unreached in this platform's own gap list. And the count: 20 declared against 60 in the YAML, forty of them pointing at four workshop boards that do not exist.
