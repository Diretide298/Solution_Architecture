accreditation-web · public audience · ACC codes
1280 × 880 frames · board index

Eight screens across two modules: five an applicant sees and three a reviewer does. This is the smallest platform in the package and the least specified — three operations across eight screens, five of which declare none at all. Drawn so the shape of the missing contract is visible: a press photographer applying to cover a Riverside Arts season, and the internal desk that decides.

"No gaps derivable" here means almost nothing was derivable. The platform page reports every operation this platform's contracts expose to its audience reaches a screen, every module spans one wave, everything is drawn, and no flow names a screen that does not exist — a clean verdict produced by there being three operations to check. Every one of the eight screens carries the same open question in the source: blocked on the Accreditation workshop. 58 requirements, zero MoM coverage. No capability or API mapping until the domain is discussed. It is one of CF-21's three unworkshopped domains, and the only platform in the package where a green report should be read as a warning.

What this board found. P11 has three operations across eight screens, and only one of them is on the right screen. decideApprovalRequest on ACC-007 is correct. listApprovalRequests — a reviewer's read — is declared on the public registration form and on the applicant's badge. getWaitTimes, the guest app's attraction-queue read, is declared on the reviewer queue — the word queue in two unrelated senses. Five screens declare nothing, including the one called Submit and the one whose whole content is server state. Two carry a purpose sentence written for a different screen. Nine operations are missing for the journey to work: create, save draft, submit, read status, withdraw, add document, get credential, list credentials, revoke credential. None of this is a drawing problem — it is 58 requirements with zero minute coverage, and this board is what that looks like once somebody tries to draw it.
