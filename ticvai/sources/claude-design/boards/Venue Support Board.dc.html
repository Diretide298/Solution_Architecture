venue-support-web · desktop · SUP codes
1280 × 880 frames · board index

Every screen the derived platform declares for P12, drawn at pack fidelity and grouped by its own four modules: Access & Availability, Overview, Conversations, Knowledge & Responses. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant. This is a staff surface, so it is drawn in the platform blues rather than the tenant's colour — an agent console is ours, not theirs, and a venue does not re-skin it.

Two things to settle before this board is built from. The screen count disagrees with its own source. platform-index.json and platform-P12.md both say 8 screens; screens/P12-support-agent-console.yaml holds 28 — SUP-001 to SUP-028. The twenty extra sit in a single Support module and each carries a workshopBoard pointing at WS17 / WS18 Customer Service Board, neither of which exists in wireframes/. The eight drawn here are the eight the platform declares; the other twenty are a separate decision, not an oversight in this board.

SUP-002 is claimed by two boards. Its wireframe.board is Dashboards Board#sup-002, where it was drawn on 26 August, with a generatedFallback anchor in the P12 board. It is drawn here too because a console missing its home screen cannot be read as a console — the frame below is the same screen, not a second one.

Authority is carried by the person, not the device
ADR-0002, and it is the whole shape of this screen. The terminal holds no rights; a shift change is a sign-in, not a handover of a logged-in window. Cursor pagination, never offset
The source says it in those words, and a queue is where it matters most: offset drifts under concurrent writes, so on a venue's busiest hour page two skips the conversation that arrived while page one was read. The count renders before the rows, because seven waiting is the number an agent acts on and the list is what they act with.

What this board found, in one place. Eight screens, seven findings. SUP-005 declares addCaseMessage, updateCase and escalateCase as onLoad — three writes fired by opening a conversation. SUP-001 reads a guest session on an agent sign-in and can revoke every session in scope. And the board-level pair at the top: 28 screens in the YAML against 8 in the platform, with twenty pointing at two workshop boards that do not exist. None of these is a drawing decision — each one is a line in the source to correct or a scope question to answer.
