guest · web · 1240 × 700 viewport
WEB codes · board index

The other guest surface. Everything the app does, done by a person who has not installed anything — on a laptop, often for somebody else, and frequently in a currency that is not the venue's. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant, the same one the mobile boards use. Where a screen has a mobile counterpart it is named, because the two must not drift: the web is not a smaller app and the app is not a shrunken site.

Complete: all 44 frames are drawn, sections 01 to 13, and every code referenced on this board resolves.

Two of P01's 46 screens are already drawn elsewhere and are not repeated here. WEB-017 My Account Dashboard is on Dashboards Board; WEB-007 Interactive Seat Selection is SEAT-3B on Seat Board 3. Both are linked from the frames that hand off to them.
