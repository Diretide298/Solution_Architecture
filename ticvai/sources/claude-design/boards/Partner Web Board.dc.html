P10 Partner Web — the reseller portal (21 screens · 8 modules)

partner-web · partner audience · PTR codes
1280 × 880 frames · board index

Twenty-one screens across eight modules — the surface a travel agent, reseller or corporate buyer works in. Northbridge Travel is the stand-in partner, selling Riverside Arts on credit terms with two sub-agents beneath it. Every frame is drawn as the screen its name promises, and names the operations that should not be reachable from it.

This is the platform where the bulk attach is most dangerous, because its audience is not staff. 105 operations across 21 screens, and the pattern is unmistakable once the contract field is read: five screens declare the same thirteen orders operations — voidOrder, holdOrder, applyManualDiscount, reprintOrder — so a voucher-download page can void an order and discount a booking. setB2bCreditLimit and overrideCreditLimit are declared on the login screen. createProduct and setPrices are declared on the partner's catalogue view, and six promotions writes on its cart. On a staff console that is untidy; on a partner portal it is a third party writing the venue's catalogue and its own credit terms.

And the count again: 21 declared, PTR-001 to PTR-051 in the YAML — the third platform in a row where the derived page under-counts its own source.

What this board found. P10 has 105 operations across 21 screens and the audience is a third party, which turns untidiness into exposure. One orders block of thirteen is declared on five screens — dashboard, allocation, booking, order history and voucher download — so the page an agent opens to print tickets can void a booking and apply a manual discount. The credit writes appear three times: setB2bCreditLimit and overrideCreditLimit on the login screen, the dashboard and the credit screen — a reseller setting its own spending power. Two more platforms' writes reached this one: eleven catalogue writes on PTR-006, including createProduct and setPrices, and six promotion writes on PTR-010. Four screens cannot do their job: Quote Management has no quote operation, Notifications can send a message and not save a preference, API Credentials declares nothing, and Sub-Agent Management is missing the platform's only two unreached operations — createPartnerUser and listPartnerUsers. Three screens are clean: Availability Search with one operation, Group Booking with four, Commission with two — and Support shares seven case operations with P12 correctly, which is the counter-example proving the rest is not merely convention. Undeclared and most important: nothing in the source records that any of the 105 is scoped to this partner's own data.
