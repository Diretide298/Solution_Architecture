All ten screens, one module, all wave 3. Every one is derived from a frame in the client packs — POS Board 6 Analytics & AI and Retail Board 6 — and every one declares the same pair, getDashboard on load and runReport on action, with one or two domain reads added. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant; the figures are a September week.

The 91-operation gap on this platform is mostly not P16's. The derived page lists 91 operations callable by this platform's audience and reaching no screen here — and the list is createLoyaltyProgramme, bulkChangePrices, setPasswordPolicy, ingestKnowledgeDocument. Those are configuration, and they belong to P08 Venue Management and P13 Venue CMS. They appear against P16 because all three share the app venue-management-web, so the gap query cannot separate them. Reading it as 91 missing analytics screens would be wrong; the number to act on is the handful named on the frames below.

What this board found. Six, and the biggest one is about the report rather than the screens. The 91-operation gap is an artefact of a shared app — P08, P13 and P16 all run on venue-management-web, so every configuration operation in the other two is attributed to analytics. Nine of ten screens declare the same two operations, which makes the dashboard definition — the cuts, the defaults, the basis — the real specification, and none of it is written down. ANL-010 has no read for a screen whose purpose is a list. ANL-009 can set an alert rule and not list them, and declares listOrders for nothing. ANL-005 depends on a costing method nobody has chosen. ANL-007 builds the CF-83 abandoned-cart segment in two clicks, and ANL-008 forecasts from eleven weeks of history against CF-45. The last two are drawn with the constraint visible rather than designed around.
