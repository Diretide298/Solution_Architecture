P04 Venue POS — Terminal and Tablet (18 screens)

staff · terminal · 1240 × 720 touch
POS codes · board index

A till is operated with one hand, under a queue, by somebody who was trained for twenty minutes. Every frame here assumes a fixed 1240 × 720 touchscreen, targets no smaller than 44px, no hover state carrying meaning, and an operator who cannot stop to read. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant, as on the guest boards.

Complete: all 18 frames are drawn. P04 lists 24 screens. Six are already drawn at pack fidelity in the POS & Frontline pack and are not repeated: POS-007 (POS-2F), POS-009 (POS-3A), POS-016 (POS-2B), POS-017 (POS-2D), POS-019 (POS-3C) and POS-020 (POS-3E). The remaining eighteen are drawn here.
Three screen ids looked like they collided. The source is consistent — the boards are not. screen-index.json and P04-point-of-sale.yaml agree on all three: POS-001 Begin Shift, POS-012 Omnichannel Order & Fulfilment Center, POS-018 Safe Drop & Cash Transfer Management — which is what the frames below draw. Nothing needs fixing at source. The clash is with the client packs, which label frames by board position (POS-2A, POS-3D) rather than by screen code; resolving a POS-0nn code against those positional labels is what produced Cash Operations Dashboard (really POS-015) and Opening Float Management. The link checker needs to stop matching screen codes against positional frame labels — that is the fix, and it is the same failure mode as the title-match join flagged on P06 Board 3.
