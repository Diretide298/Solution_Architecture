What Phase 2 says about the package
Half of P08 is one pattern
172 of the 363 declare split, and once the twenty mislabels are corrected it is 192 — a list, a detail panel and a search box, drawn once at BP-001. The variety in this platform is in its data, not its layouts.
127 screens nobody wrote states for
A third of the platform carries statesDerived: true — the flag that says the empty, error and no-access copy came from the screen's name and operations rather than from a person. Every defect on the three earlier boards came out of that flag.
One screen has no route
BO-235 Access Attribute Catalog is the re-signature from the vocabulary pass — it leaves BP-006 for a list and cannot be routed until the YAML is patched. It is the only one of 363.
This board is generated from the platform file, so it stays true if the file changes. The routing is computed from each screen's declared template and component set against the twenty drawn frames — patch the YAMLs per notes/atlas-handoff.md, re-parse, and the twenty mislabel rows move from BP-010 to BP-001 on their own. The 27 flagged rows are the build order: a screen whose declaration is wrong gets built wrong, whichever frame it points at.
