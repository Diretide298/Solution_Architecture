Seven frames, and a cleanup that stopped after one screen

The two clusters that closed the pattern gap. Queue is four screens the build plan wanted drawn as integration rather than implementation, and ADR-0012 already settles that: adaptor-first, a named vendor is one driver behind a stable inbound shape. Resource management is three screens carrying 47 requirements, drawn as they stand with the missing surface written down rather than invented. Both clusters turned up the same thing the vocabulary pass did — the defect is in what the screens declare, not in how they look.

BO-003 was cleaned on 24 August and the check was never re-run. Fifteen order operations came off it — create an order, apply a discount, exchange lines, refund — and its own notes record how they were found: deriving emptyNoAccess produced "needs ORDER_CREATE to create orders" on a screen called Queue Integration Setup, a sentence accurate to the data and absurd about the screen. The other three queue screens still carry the residue. BO-005 Queue Monitor declares nineteen operations of which twelve are campaign management, and its empty state reads "needs MARKETING_MANAGE, MARKETING_SEND to manage marketings". Same absurdity, second screen, still there.

CF-125 Three screens is not a bounded context 47 requirements · 3 screens · 5 named below

The three frames above are drawn as declared, and drawing them is what makes the gap legible: every one of the five missing screens is something the three already reference and none of them owns. A first-class bookable resource needs a type registry, availability rules, conflict handling and a maintenance state. This is the same shape as the seatp finding — design and specification disagreeing about whether something exists — except here the specification is the one asking.

What these seven frames turned up
The 24 August cleanup was never finished
BO-003 lost 15 order operations. BO-001 still carries 6 catalogue, BO-002 carries 4 catalogue and 2 seating, BO-005 carries 12 marketing. 24 operations across three screens, all the same class of defect, all findable by the check that already exists.
A sixth vocabulary defect
BO-096 declares template: calendar, which is not in the schema enum, so it fails CI as written. BO-097 declares an offline state on a platform not marked offlineCapable — correct for the screen, wrong for the platform flag.
The vendor question was smaller than the plan thought
ADR-0012 already answers it for three of the four screens: adaptor-first, and a vendor is one of seven sources. CF-33a decides whether BO-005 exists and nothing else. One screen at risk, not four.
Phase 1 is now the eight strays. BP-023's scanTarget on Interactive Seat Selection is still unchecked — a guest picking a seat is not scanning anything, so either the component is wrong or the screen is two screens. After that, Phase 2: P08 has 363 screens and no board, and these seven frames plus the eight on Pattern Boards cover the patterns its screens declare.
