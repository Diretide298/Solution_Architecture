P09 TICVAI Web — the platform console (37 screens · 9 modules)

ticvai-web · Softlabs audience · ADM codes
1280 × 880 frames · board index

Thirty-seven screens across nine modules — the console Softlabs runs the platform from. Tenants, licensing, releases, cells, and the one screen that holds the AI providers. Riverside Arts appears here as one tenant among several, which is the difference between this platform and every other board in the set.

Two operation blocks account for most of this platform's 110 declarations, and one of them is destructive. Seven subscription operations — getCell, getCellHealth, getCellCapacity, listCellJobs, updateCellTier, decommissionCell, cancelDecommission — are declared identically on eight screens, including a WAF policy view, a backup status page and an archival job monitor. Each of those can tear down a tenant's cell, and none of them has a WAF, backup or archival operation of its own. A second block of six tenant reads appears on ten screens, carrying getSsoConfig onto a usage-metering page.

And the audit log cannot read the audit log. ADM-004 Platform Audit Log declares exactly one operation: listAiInteractions. Fourth platform in a row where the count disagrees with its source: 37 declared, ADM-001 to ADM-102 in the YAML.
