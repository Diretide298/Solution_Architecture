EMP codes · board index

A venue-owned handheld, held in one hand, in daylight, by somebody halfway through a twelve-hour shift. Dark theme from the employee-app reference, the Home · Tasks · Scan · AI · More shell with the raised centre action, targets no smaller than 44px, and every screen answering what it does when the signal drops. Riverside Arts is the stand-in tenant, as on the other boards.

P06 lists 66 screens; 21 are already drawn elsewhere and are not repeated. The sixteen Floor Service screens EMP-051–EMP-066 sit at pack fidelity on FnB Board 4 and FnB Board 5, EMP-030 Venue map is Seat-4C, and the four Stock on the Floor screens EMP-067–EMP-070 are FNB-5E, RET-4H, RET-4J and INV-10. That leaves 45 generated screens across three boards: this board takes the first 16 — getting on shift, scanning, and the task loop. Gate operations, the assistant, comms and emergency are the twelve on Board 2; people, incidents and settings are the seventeen on Board 3. 45 + 21 = 66.
These definitions were derived from the 14 August board, not written. Purposes and navigation are real; components and states are inferred. Two screens carry bulk-attach residue, and both turn out to be the same defect: a whole contract attached at once, the shape CF-114 caught on EMP-036.

EMP-003’s seventeen operations are the entire shift contract (twelve, including openShift, closeShift, reopenShift, suspendShift, recordNoSale) plus the entire maintenance contract (five). It is the same set POS-001 Begin Shift holds, where a till legitimately needs it — createCashMovement was already cut from here on 18 August for exactly that reason, and the other eleven have the same provenance. The frame below shows what a home screen can honestly do.

EMP-007 Handover notes holds the workforce announcement contract in full — the identical four operations on EMP-037, EMP-039 and EMP-047. The name is not the problem; the contract is missing. A note addressed to the next shift is not an all-staff announcement, and no handoverNote operation exists anywhere in the package — the same gap as EMP-041 Training. Drawn as what it must do.
