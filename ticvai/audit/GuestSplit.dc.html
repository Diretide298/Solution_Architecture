Cluster 01 · the largest single overlap

Guest Web and Guest App are one product

Twenty screens carry the same name on both platforms, and the pairs go further than names: on every one of the twenty, both screens declare exactly the same contract operations. P01 is 46 screens and P02 is 63; the overlap is a third of the mobile app. The purpose lines differ — only one pair of twenty shares a sentence — but nothing in either definition says what a guest can actually do on one that they cannot do on the other.

The three that are genuinely mobile

Only a handful of P02 screens depend on being an installed app: GST-055 Dynamic QR Ticket (a rotating code the web cannot hold), GST-059 Plan My Day – In Progress (location through the day) and the push-notification path behind GST-030. Those justify a native app. Twenty duplicated storefront screens do not.

Two of these are not screens

Multi-Currency & Pricing (WEB-035, GST-044) is a property of every price on every screen, not a place a guest goes. Lost & Found appears a third time as EMP-028 “Lost & found” in the staff app, which is the only one with a person on the other side of it. Both are worth re-checking before they get drawn.
