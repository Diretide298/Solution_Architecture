Tier 2 · recurring by construction

The same job, once per platform

These are not accidental collisions. Each is a capability that was given its own screens on every platform that touches it — so the same report engine, stock ledger and kitchen queue are specified three, four and five times over, each time with slightly different operations and a different name.

One report engine, twenty-five surfaces
P04 · P08 · P09 · P10 · P12 · P15 · P16

Twenty-five screens call runReport, getDashboard or getFinancialReport. Nine of the ten P16 Venue Analytics screens are among them — the platform is a set of saved report configurations, not a distinct product. Four of them are the whole of P08's reporting section under different titles, and BO-029 Report Builder already builds the rest.

P16 — nine of ten are a report
ANL-002Sales, Revenue & Channel ANL-003Operational Performance ANL-004Product Performance ANL-005Cost, Margin & Profitability ANL-006Inventory & Waste Intelligence ANL-007Guest & Conversion Intelligence

Every one of these six is getDashboard + runReport plus at most three operations that fetch the figures. The difference between them is the report they load. ANL-010 is the exception on the platform — it calls neither.

The kitchen queue, in three places

P15 is a ten-screen platform for the kitchen. It uses 24 operations and 17 of them already appear on other platforms — nine in P08's F&B section, seven at the point of sale.

KIT-002Kitchen Display System BO-046Kitchen Display POS-022Send to Kitchen BO-135Order Routing & KDS/Printer Rules BO-134Kitchen & Preparation Stations KIT-005Kitchen Station Workload & Dynamic Routing

KIT-005 and BO-134 are the same two operations exactly — the kitchen configures its stations from the kitchen, and the back office configures them again. Setup belongs in one place; the kitchen queue itself belongs on the kitchen screen.

One people directory, three owners

Three screens on three platforms declare exactly listPrincipals, getPrincipal, createPrincipal, updatePrincipal — the same four operations over the same records.

BO-053Staff Directory ADM-020Platform User Directory PTR-003Profile & Company Details

The scope differs — a venue's staff, the platform's users, a partner's own people — and that is exactly the shape of one screen with a scope, not three screens with three sets of empty and permission states.

The counter-example, so the method is checkable

P06's Floor Service module looks like the same kind of duplicate — ten screens over restaurant tables, sitting next to P08's F&B section. It is not. The two share two operations out of twenty-nine each. Table service is genuinely a different job from F&B configuration, and this module survives the audit unchanged. The same test is what puts the other clusters on this page.
