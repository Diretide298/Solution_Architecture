Please find attached WebUIRev3_28thSep, the updated TICVAI guest booking prototypes. They cover every point in the Rev 3.0 feedback from 25 September. Where your document included an example screenshot, the screen now follows that example.

What's in the folder
- TICVAI Guest Booking v2 (single file).html: the website. Double-click to open. It takes about 15 seconds to load the first time.
- TICVAI Guest Booking Mobile v2 (single file).html: the mobile app. Booking inside the app now runs the same live engine as the website, so every flow below works on mobile too. The earlier clickable screens are still under Account → All screens → Rev 3 feedback.
- CLIENT-RESPONSE-REV3-25SEP.md: our response to each point, where to see it, and which setting controls it.

Main changes
- Performances: date first, then time, then tickets. Long lists page and filter by morning, afternoon and evening.
- Stadium and theatre: Flow 1 (fixed date, straight to the seat map) and Flow 2 (date and time first), with a pop-up or inline option. The seat view box can sit on the right, left, top or bottom, and there is a time bar above the map. The theatre has its own seat map, and picking the show and the seats happen on one page.
- Dining: table booking now completes, and F&B items have add-ons and modifiers.
- Basket: fixed right, left, slide-in, slide-up or floating icon. It stays on the right in Arabic.
- Help me choose: a banner under the products, "Choose from the experiences above or let us help you decide", opens the questions.
- Swim consent: a pop-up asks "Are you able to swim?" once the guest picks a water park session. It can be turned on or off.
- Cabanas: booked straight from the water park map on the first screen. There are 34 numbered cabanas, each showing how many guests it seats and whether it's sold out, with a remaining-time counter.
- Surf sessions: a four-day calendar, and both filters show a description under each option.
- Experiences, meeting rooms (House of Wisdom style), info-only products, category → subcategory tickets, guided tours by language, a location switcher and the Quick Tour.
- Transport: built to the RTA layout. It has 3 Simple Steps, the One-way / Multi-Trip / Favourite tabs, one search row, and the Search Trips and Next Available Trip buttons. The chosen trip shows a price card and a street map of the route. The map needs an internet connection.
- Every flow was checked for repeated steps, and duplicates were removed.
- Arabic: the interface text added in this round now has Arabic.

All new options are in Config → Booking rules (Rev 3). Sessions can be added, edited or closed from Config → Sessions.
