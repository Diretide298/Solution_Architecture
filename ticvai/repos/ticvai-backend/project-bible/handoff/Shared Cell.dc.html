A museum on its own private setup costs $948 a month — more than three times its share of a shared one, for a venue serving about one guest per second. Across two hundred customers that decision is $97,000 a month against $190,000. Same software, same performance, twice the cost.

The trade, stated rather than assumed

Blast radius is the cell — every tenant in it. scope_path partitioning is correctness, not isolation: a bug reaches one tenant’s rows; an outage reaches all of them. Data is kept properly separate so nobody can see anyone else’s, but availability is shared even though privacy is not. And one thing worth knowing before it is promised — restoring one customer’s data to last Tuesday would restore everybody’s. That belongs in a tenant contract rather than in an architecture document nobody reads.
