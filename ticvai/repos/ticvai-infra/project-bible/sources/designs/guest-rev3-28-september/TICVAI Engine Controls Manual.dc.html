TICVAI engine controls

Operator manual · guest booking web app

The engine controls panel re-skins and re-wires the guest booking app while it is running. Every screenshot in this manual was taken from the live build, one control at a time, so each pair shows the same screen before and after a single change.

The panel is a demo and configuration surface. Guests never see it, and nothing in it is saved to a guest's booking — it is there so you can show any venue, any brand and any flow from one URL.

Opening the panel

Open it from the dark square button at the left end of the navigation bar — the single bar that carries the venue lockup, the guest navigation, the account controls and the venue's own call to action. The page behind it dims; the drawer itself scrolls independently. Close it with the × in its top corner or by clicking the dimmed page.

Controls are grouped in five blocks, in this order: Venue type, Booking flow, Brand, Flow, Shape & background, Layout & locale.

Every change applies immediately, with no reload and without losing the guest's place in the flow. Reset to defaults sits at the bottom of the drawer.

1 · Venue type

Options: Theme park · Water park · Stadium · Theatre · Dining
Default: Theme park

The venue buttons switch the whole tenant. Each one carries its own logo and lockup, colour palette, typeface, photography, section copy, ticket tiers, add-ons and step names — a water park shows sessions and wristbands, a stadium shows fixtures and stands, a restaurant shows tables and covers. The booking flow list below also re-scopes to the flows that venue offers.

2 · Booking flow

Options: 34 flows in total, listed per venue
Theme park: Dated day pass · Timed entry / capacity slots · Multi-park combo · Annual pass / membership · Fast-track upgrade · Height & age eligibility gate · Group / school booking

The flow decides which steps exist and what each one asks for. A day pass runs Tickets → Extras → Details → Payment → Confirmed; an eligibility gate adds rider requirements and a guardian waiver; a group booking replaces card payment with an invoice and drops the extras step; a waitlist or offer flow ends in a hold rather than a sale. Changing the flow restarts the guest at step one, because the steps themselves are different.

3 · Brand

Brand controls change the skin only. Prices, availability, steps and copy stay exactly where they were, so you can hand the same booking to a different tenant mid-demo.

Palette

Options: Auto (per venue) + 18 named palettes — Sunset amber, Deep teal, Stadium red, Playhouse plum, Saffron cream, Midnight ink, Paper mono, Racing red, Dive cyan, Kids playground, Arena violet, Cinema crimson, Desert gold, Ticvai blue, Ticvai night, Souk terracotta, Slate lime, Mobile app · Nocturne dark / light
Default: Auto (per venue)

Sets brand, accent, ink and surface colours together, and decides whether the app runs light or dark. On Auto, each venue uses its own palette. A named palette overrides all five venues, which is how you check a tenant's colours against an unfamiliar flow.

Typeface

Options: Auto (venue brand) · Archivo / IBM Plex Sans · Instrument Serif / Public Sans · Manrope only · Inter Tight (mobile app)
Default: Auto (venue brand)

Swaps the display and body pairing across every screen. Auto keeps each venue's own fonts. The pairings are sized to hold the same layout, so switching never reflows a card out of its frame.

Surfaces

Options: Glass · Solid  |  Default: Glass

Glass gives cards, the cart and overlays a translucent fill with a 20px backdrop blur. Solid replaces that with an opaque fill. Use Solid for embeds over busy host pages, for low-powered kiosk hardware, and whenever a screenshot needs flat colour.

Brand logo & Logo palette

Brand logo: Venue palette · Ticvai · Ferrari World · Explorers · Majid Al Futtaim · Deep Dive Dubai
Logo palette: Light · Dark · Duotone  |  Defaults: Venue palette, Light

Brand logo sets which lockup sits in the navigation bar and on the powered-by strip. Venue palette uses the venue's own mark; the other five drop a real partner lockup in its place, which is how you check a mark against a bar it was not drawn for.

Logo palette decides which reading of that lockup's colours is pulled into the theme — light, dark or duotone. It re-reads the artwork rather than recolouring it, so a mark with fixed brand colours looks the same in all three while the page tone behind it changes.

Sahli mascot

Toggle  |  Default: on

Shows or hides Sahli, the AI concierge. On, the mascot sits in a rounded frame in the corner, reacts to what the guest is doing, and opens the concierge panel. Off, the concierge is still available but appears as a plain button with no character art — the setting to use for tenants who bring their own mascot.

4 · Flow

Flow controls change how the guest chooses: how choices are presented, how many steps there are, and how seats are picked.

Card layout

Options: Stacked rows · Split rows · Cards across · Poster cards  |  Default: Stacked rows

Sets how the choice cards on the first step are built. Stacked rows put a thumbnail, name and price on one line and expand for guest numbers. Split rows give the image more room. Cards across lays them out as a grid. Poster cards lead with full-width imagery, for titles and shows that sell on the artwork.

The same setting drives the browse pages: the What's on results list and the rail on Discover. In the two grid modes the results wrap into a grid and every card in it is the same height, whatever the length of its title; in the two row modes they stay a single column and the Discover rail scrolls sideways.

The same four settings on What's on

In both grid modes every card is forced to the same height, so a two-line title never leaves one card taller than the ones beside it, and the Book button sits on the same line across the row.

Card size

Options: Compact · Standard · Large · Extra large  |  Default: Compact

Scales the choice cards without changing their layout: thumbnail size, padding, title and the weight of the price all move together, on the booking steps and on the browse pages alike. Compact fits more above the fold; Extra large suits kiosks and walk-up counters where the screen is further away.

Quantities on add-ons

Toggle  |  Default: on

On, every add-on carries a stepper, so a guest can take four parking spaces or two photo packs. Off, add-ons become single checkboxes — one per booking. Turn it off for venues whose extras are per-booking rather than per-guest.

Seat picker

Options: Stadium bowl (zones → seats) · Zones, then seats · Zones only (capacity) · Seats only
Default: Stadium bowl. Applies to venues with a seated step — stadium and theatre.

Stadium bowl draws the real bowl: pick a stand, then zoom into its seats. Zones, then seats keeps the two-stage choice but lists the zones instead of drawing them. Zones only sells by zone capacity with steppers and no seat numbers — right for general admission and standing. Seats only drops straight to a single seat grid, which suits small rooms.

Map view

Options: 2D · 3D  |  Default: 3D

2D is the flat plan: fast, legible, and the safer choice on weak hardware. 3D is an isometric bowl the guest can orbit and zoom, with clickable stands and a crowd; the page keeps scrolling normally around it. Guests can also switch between them from the toggle on the seating step, along with the sport and concert configurations.

Extras step

Options: Auto (per flow) · Always · Never  |  Default: Auto (per flow)

Auto follows the flow: invoiced group bookings, waitlists and offer holds skip extras, everything else shows them. Always forces the step in; Never removes it, and the add-ons are still reachable from the cart's often-added panel. The step count in the progress bar follows.

5 · Shape & background

These four are the fastest way to make the engine sit inside a host site it was not designed for: page tone, corner radius, button shape and spacing.

Background

Options: Auto, plus 28 tones — brand tints and washes, neutral papers (Paper white, Warm sand, Cool grey, Greige, Bone), accent tints, and darks (Near black, Charcoal, Deep navy, Forest night, Plum night, Espresso)
Default: Auto

Sets the page behind the cards. Auto follows the palette. Brand tints and washes pull the tenant colour into the page; the neutrals keep it out of the way; the darks flip the app without changing the palette's accents. Text and card contrast are recalculated with the tone, so ink stays readable on every option.

Corner radius

Range: 0–22px  |  Default: 10px

One slider drives the radius of cards, inputs, chips, images and the cart. Buttons keep their own shape, set by the next control.

Buttons

Options: Solid · Outline · Pill  |  Default: Solid

Applies to every primary and secondary button, including the cart's continue button and the payment actions. Outline keeps the brand colour but drops the fill; Pill rounds fully regardless of the corner radius.

Density

Options: Compact · Standard · Roomy  |  Default: Compact

Scales padding and the gaps between blocks across every step. It does not change type size or hit targets, so touch targets stay at least 44px on Compact.

6 · Layout & locale

Where the engine runs and who it runs for: page or embed, cart position, progress style, currency and reading direction.

Embed mode

Options: Full page · Embedded  |  Default: Full page

Full page is the standalone booking site: venue header, hero and footer. Embedded drops all of it and renders only the engine, for dropping into a tenant's own page. The powered-by strip, progress and secure-checkout note stay in both.

Checkout layout

Options: Cart sidebar · Single column  |  Default: Cart sidebar

Cart sidebar keeps the running total, VAT and continue button beside the step. Single column moves the cart below the content, full width — the layout to use for narrow embeds and for mobile-first demos.

Progress

Options: Bar · Numbered steps · Dots  |  Default: Bar

Sets the step indicator under the powered-by strip. Numbered steps name every step and let a guest jump back to a completed one. Bar and Dots are quieter, for short flows and for embeds where the host page already shows its own progress.

Currency

Options: AED · SAR · USD · INR · GBP  |  Default: AED

Converts every price, add-on, subtotal, VAT line and total at a fixed demo rate and relabels them. Amounts are rounded to whole units, so a converted total will not always match the sum of its converted parts to the last unit.

Right to left

Toggle  |  Default: off

Mirrors the whole engine: reading order, cart side, date strips, steppers, progress and the concierge. Copy stays in English, so this is a layout check rather than a translation.

Reset, and what carries over

Reset to defaults, at the bottom of the drawer, returns every control in all four groups to the values listed in this manual. It leaves the venue and the flow as they are.

Brand, shape and locale controls never touch the guest's progress: a cart built on the theme park survives a switch to Desert gold, Solid surfaces or right-to-left. Venue type, booking flow and seat picker do change what the steps are, so they restart the guest at step one.

Settings live in the session only. A reload returns the engine to its defaults, which makes the panel safe to hand to someone else mid-demo.
