Deployment · a flash sale gets its own environment

Run the burst environment, and watch where it fails

A museum and a stadium are the same software and completely different machines. Steady state sizes nothing: every tier spends its day between 1 and 50 requests a second, and the entire question is the window. Sixty thousand seats over two hours is 1,045 requests a second; the same seats sold out in twenty minutes is 6,270. This page runs that window — queueing, autoscaling, lease contention, reconciliation and cost — and names every place it breaks.
