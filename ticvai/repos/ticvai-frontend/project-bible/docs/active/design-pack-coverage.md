# Design pack coverage: the 40 "undrafted" PDFs

> **Purpose:** One row per PDF that `sources/packs-index.json` lists under `undraftedDocuments`: is it scope, what in the package already covers it, what is missing and what to do about it
> **Owner:** Chinmay
> **Status:** Decided 29 September 2026 (readiness close-out, group QS). Supersedes the scope half of `undrafted-packs-scope-20-september.md`

**"Undrafted" means only that `draft-pack-operations.py` never wrote an operation citing the pack.**
`index-packs.py` sets `drafted` from `<pack>, page N` citations, so a pack built by hand, or through the
board renders, reads as undrafted. Of the 40 PDFs, **14 are fully covered, 5 are covered with a small
residual, 6 are real gaps, 1 is deferred by a MoM, 13 are renders or references, and 1 is the RFP itself.**

**Scope rule used** (`sources/README.md`): a pack is design, not scope. Each verdict cites the RFP
(`specifications/rfp/`), the requirement matrix (`requirements/Ticvai_matrix_20260621_2.xlsx`) or a MoM.
**Every pack here traces to one of them, so none stays a client question.** The one deferral, AI
forecasting, is the client's own decision (MoM 14 Aug item 1).

**Coverage was measured on 29 September** from `screens/P*.yaml` (`source.pack`, `apis`, `boardFrames`,
`derivedFrom`), `contracts/*/*.yaml` (operations and pack citations) and `flows/`. Operation estimates
for gaps are ours and give a range. Where a 20 September upper bound exists, the row quotes it.

**The 20 September estimate was 638 new operations. It falls to about 103-172 for four reasons:**
- A ticket type is a catalogue `ProductKind`, not a separate thing, so the 120 estimated for it drops to 5-15.
- The F&B, Retail and Inventory boards came in through the renders (`wireframes/reference/*Board*`), not through their text packs.
- BI boards 5-8 are a domain filter, not new screens.
- The AI forecasting and optimisation boards (AI Forecasting, Resource board 8, VQ board 4, Inventory boards 5 and 7) wait for data, per MoM 14 Aug.

**Make-or-break for the client: none.** Nothing here breaks a law, needs a client-only name or
credential, or changes what guests pay.
