# The four BI domain boards, and why they are a filter rather than forty screens

**9 September 2026.** `Unified_BI_Reporting_and_AI_Analytics_Platform_Reference.pdf` specifies ten boards of ten screens. **Sixty of them are screens in `screens/P16-venue-analytics.yaml`; the forty listed here are not, and this is the record of that.**

## The rule this follows was already the platform's

P16 exists because 26 board screens were collapsed to nine. Its own platform note says why:

> **A domain is a filter on an analytics screen, not a copy of it.** Building three sets means maintaining one screen three times and watching them drift, and the drift is invisible until two dashboards disagree about last month.

Boards 5 to 8 are that shape exactly. Each is one domain — Sales, Finance, Operations, Customer — and each opens with a command centre, runs through per-subject analytics and closes with an AI intelligence screen. **The four boards are the same six screens under four domain names.** Drawing all forty would rebuild the duplication the collapse removed, and it would be a second set of dashboards for the same figures.

## What this is not

**It is not a decision that the client asked for less than they asked for.** Every one of the forty is in `sources/workshop/pack.json` with its purpose, its measures and its acceptance condition, joined and searchable; the parse reconciles at 770 screens across 77 boards and would fail if any were dropped. What is absent is forty *screen definitions*, and the claim being made is that a domain selector on the boards that were drawn serves the same requirement.

**Whether that claim holds is a question for the client**, and it is the one to put to them: does Finance need its own reconciliation workspace, or a Finance filter on the reporting screens? Board 6 is the likeliest to need its own — gateway reconciliation and ERP matching are not a filtered view of a revenue dashboard, and four of its ten screens are named for work that no other board does.

**And the filter the claim rests on does not exist yet on fifty-nine of the sixty screens.** See *What to do with this* below; it is the first thing to settle, because until it is, this file describes an intention rather than a state.

Boards 2, 3, 4 and 10 are configuration and governance rather than dashboards — a report builder is not a domain view of anything — and board 9 is the AI layer over all of them. **None of the six collapses into another**, which is the test the four domain boards fail.

## What to do with this

  Put the Finance question to the client first. If board 6 needs its own screens, it is ten more, not forty.

  **The filter this argument depends on is on one screen of the sixty.** `ANL-011 Executive Command Center` carries the pack's `Global Filters` — *Organization / Tenant, Country, Site / Venue, Attraction, Business Unit, Date / Period, Sales Channel, Product Category, Currency* — and `Business Unit` is the domain. **The other fifty-nine do not have it**, because the pack does not give it to them, and nothing has been added to make the argument look better than it is.

  What the pack does say is weaker and worth stating exactly: `Revenue Pulse` displays *Revenue by Business Unit* and adds that the breakdown *"shall include, where applicable: Ticketing | F&B | Retail | Membership | B2B | Add-ons | Parking | Other Services"*. So **the domain is a dimension the dashboards break down by, not yet a control they are filtered by.** That is enough to say the data is the same data. It is not enough to say the screens already do what boards 5 to 8 ask for.

  So the collapse is a proposal with a condition attached, and the condition is unmet today: either the domain filter goes onto the analytics screens as a design decision — which is an addition to the pack and should be recorded as one, not slipped in by a generator — or the four boards get their screens. **Deciding by leaving it is deciding for the second while the package reads as the first**, which is the failure this file exists to prevent.

  If the answer is that all forty are wanted, `tools/derive-pack-screens.py` draws them: remove the four entries from `BOARD_PLACEMENT` and re-run. Nothing else has to change.
