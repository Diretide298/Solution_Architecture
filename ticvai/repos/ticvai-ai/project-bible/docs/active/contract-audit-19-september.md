# Contract audit — every contract against the four things that must agree with it

> Generated by `tools/audit-contracts.py`. **Contracts sit at the top of the chain**, so a
> contract that has drifted produces a screen layer that cannot be bound and a gap list
> proposing operations that already exist under another name.

| verdict | meaning | what to do |
|---|---|---|
| **JOIN OR DRIFT** | many unused operations **and** many unserved board screens | **sort its operations runtime vs authoring.** A clean split means a missing join and no contract work; a scatter means real drift |
| undersized | far fewer operations than the boards describe | author, guided by the matrix |
| stale | a quarter or more of its operations reach no screen | decide each: superseded, renamed, or still wanted |
| matrix gaps | the matrix expects more than it carries | close against the requirement |
| aligned | none of the above | leave it |
