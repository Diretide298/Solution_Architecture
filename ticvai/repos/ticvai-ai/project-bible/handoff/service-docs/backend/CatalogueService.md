## Why this is a service

**What is sold, at what price, in which seat.** Promotions and seating are folded in because both are pricing and availability against the same product — a promotion that cannot see a price list and a seat that cannot see a product are two round trips on the hot path of every sale. **`pricing` joined it on 20 September**, which closes decision 5 of the schema merge — it took all three `pricing` tables and said the schema needed an owner, and that stayed open because the tables had no operations and a service is inferred from the contract that declares them. The operations were written into `catalogue.yaml`, beside the price lists and products a dynamic rule adjusts: a rule that reads `catalogue.price_list` and `catalogue.product` to compute a price belongs on the side of the boundary those live on, or every evaluation is a cross-service call on the sale path.
