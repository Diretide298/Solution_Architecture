# Readiness close-out

> **Purpose:** every operation that waited for sign-off, what we decided on 29 September, and the evidence  
> **Owner:** Chinmay  
> **Status:** Record. Generated by `tools/build-closeout-register.py` from `handoff/readiness-closeout.json`

## The decision

**Answer every readiness question from the client's own material (MoM, RFP and requirements, design packs and boards); where no workshop was held, decide from our build plan. Keep only make-or-break questions for the client: (a) law, regulator or third-party contract, (b) a name, account, credential or vendor only the client has, (c) money the client's customers pay or are refunded that their commercial team would reverse.**

Decided by Chinmay on 29 September 2026. 579 operations had been drafted from the client's design packs and waited a month for a backend engineer to say "yes, that is the shape" (`tools/build-provisional-review.py`).

## How it was done

1. **One brief, one rule** (`audit/ticvai/steps/Q/BRIEF.md`). Sources, in authority order: the minutes (`sources/mom/`, digested in `sources/mom-decisions.json`), the RFP and requirements, then the design packs and boards. Decisions already taken (the 77 audit decisions, the rev 3 decisions) were followed, not reopened.
2. **Four agents, one set of contracts each**, working at the same time:
   - QA: access and approvals
   - QB: catalogue and subscription
   - QC: promotions and orders
   - QD: marketing-crm

   Each read every operation against its cited pack page and the minutes, fixed the shape where the evidence said so, then either agreed it or kept it open.
3. **Agreeing an operation** removes `x-ticvai-provisional` and adds `x-ticvai-agreed: '29 September: <evidence>'`. The operation's description ends with an "Agreed (decided 29 September, readiness close-out)" paragraph saying what changed.
4. **Keeping one open** leaves it provisional with `x-ticvai-make-or-break: '<question>'`.
5. **Screens followed the contracts.** Summary tiles moved into `*Summary` schemas, filters became query parameters, and example values stopped being fields. About 1,300 screen bindings were repointed (group RB), and `check-bindings` ended better than before the close-out.
6. **Gaps the review found were filled:** 99 operations were added, by groups K1, K2 and K3 from the Venue Management close-out, plus QD and the coordinator. Group BN bound them to their screens.
7. **Checks** after each group: the YAML parses, line endings are kept, and `check-states`, `check-config-scope`, `check-bindings`, `check-screens` and `check-flows` pass. Then the full package refresh.

The working files (briefs, per-group results, review packets, apply scripts) are in `audit/ticvai/steps/Q/` on the coordinator's machine. The step is logged as S006 in `audit/ticvai/steps.jsonl`.
