## The questions in full

### R038: Approved dependencies

**Question.** Will you approve a list of third-party libraries each runtime may use (backend, frontend, infrastructure), and who on your side signs off an addition?

**Why it matters.** The setup bible's dependencies.md is marked 'Status: Stub' and its 'Approved by runtime' section reads 'To be filled as the stack settles'. The DevOps lead's starter inherits the gap.

**Blocks.** SETUP-CI, SETUP-ENV and SETUP-OBS cannot enforce a dependency rule, so every library a developer adds is unreviewed.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** We draft the list from what the starters already ship (Serilog, OpenTelemetry to OTLP, health checks) and you approve it. Anything else needs one named approver on your side.

**Client confirms.** Client IT / architecture

### R057: Cloud and CI platform

**Question.** Which cloud provider and region do we deploy to (open item CF-64: AWS or Azure), and which CI system runs the pipelines?

**Why it matters.** CF-64 still reads 'AWS or Azure, pending DESC', yet the old infra scaffold hard-codes azurerm. The bible uses GitLab wording ('Merge Requests') while the repos ship .github/workflows. The six DevOps SETUP tickets carry one-line descriptions because none of these choices is made.

**Blocks.** SETUP-CI, SETUP-ENV (Terraform, cell mapping), SETUP-DB and SETUP-OBS. Environments cannot be provisioned until the provider is fixed.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** Azure in a UAE region, since the existing Terraform scaffold already targets azurerm, and GitHub Actions, since the repositories already ship workflows there.

**Client confirms.** Client IT / infrastructure

### R065: Payment sandbox access

**Question.** When will we have sandbox credentials for Stripe and Network International, and who on your side owns getting them?

**Why it matters.** F01, the WEB-012 screen and integrations/payment.md all list the sandbox credentials as outstanding, and no task delivers them. The declined, unknown-outcome and reconcile branches of WEB-012 and SVC-ORDER-PAYMENT-1 can only be tested against a fake. inquirePaymentStatus also says a background reconciler runs 'past a threshold' that is never given.

**Blocks.** Testing guest online payment end to end (WEB-012, SVC-ORDER-PAYMENT-1) and the recovery flows. The provider adapter cannot be finished against the real APIs.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** A named owner delivers both sandboxes by a fixed date, which we add to the plan as a task. For the reconciler threshold, we propose using each provider's own recommended status-poll interval. Confirm or correct it.

**Client confirms.** Client finance / payments owner

### R070: Row-level security coverage

**Question.** We propose that every tenant table holding operational data or personal data gets row-level security, either through its own venue or scope column or through its parent row. Only genuine shared reference data is exempt. Confirm or correct.

**Why it matters.** 920-row-level-security.sql puts a policy on 313 of 584 tenant tables. The other ~270 are labelled 'reference data', but they include all 4 pii tables, identity.principal, orders.order_line, wallet.gift_card and games.credit_ledger. permission-resolution.md 7.1 says every tenant-scoped table enforces RLS. We are fixing this ourselves, but it changes what one venue's staff can see of another's data.

**Blocks.** The 'row-level security holds' Done-when on every MIG and SVC ticket. As the DDL stands, it cannot be met for these tables.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** Confirm the proposal as written: no tenant or PII table is exempt from RLS.

**Client confirms.** Client security

### STATE-MEDIA: Media library lifecycle

**Why it matters.** The media state model (states/media.yaml) calls ready, quarantined and archived terminal, yet updateMediaAsset moves between them (quarantined->ready, ready->archived, archived->ready). ### STATE-SUBSCRIPTION: Subscription reactivation

**Question.** Can a cancelled subscription be reactivated (setSubscription back to active), or must the tenant start a new subscription?

**Why it matters.** states/subscription.yaml marks cancelled as terminal but also lists cancelled->active through setSubscription. A reactivation keeps history and entitlements on one record; a new subscription starts clean. The package records neither decision.

**Blocks.** SubscriptionService billing and the tenant plan screens; check-states fails on it.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** Reactivation within the paid period is allowed on the same record; after the period ends the subscription expires and a new one is required.

### R-M18-15: Marketing opt-in at guest checkout

**Question.** May a guest who checks out without an account opt in to marketing at checkout, and does that opt-in, given against a verified contact, count as marketing consent?

**Why it matters.** The 18 September minutes (M18-15) asked for the opt-in beside the terms at checkout. The package recorded marketing consent only against a guest profile, so a guest-checkout opt-in had nowhere to go, and a pre-ticked or inferred consent is what PDPL forbids.

**Blocks.** WEB-012, GST-009, orders checkoutCart, marketing consent records.

**Decided (28 September, our recommendation).** An unticked opt-in beside the terms, one per channel and purpose; checkoutCart carries marketingConsents[] (never defaulted to granted), bound to the order and the verified contact; marketing recordCheckoutConsents writes each ticked entry as a consent record with source checkout and attaches it to the profile when the contact matches one. Nothing is sent without it. Kept make-or-break on whether a guest-checkout opt-in is sufficient consent under PDPL.
