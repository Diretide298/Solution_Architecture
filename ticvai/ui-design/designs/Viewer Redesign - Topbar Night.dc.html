Offline — cannot start

The storefront is a web surface with no local bundle. Availability and pricing are read live, and selling from a cached catalogue to an anonymous browser would oversell within minutes. This is the deliberate opposite of the POS position (ADR-0013): a POS has a known workstation, a shift and a lease; a browser has none of those.
