Credit is the difference

A partner spends before paying. That puts MFA on the login, a primary read on the balance and a settlement trail behind every order — none of which the guest surfaces need.

Allocation is not availability

PTR-005 and PTR-007 answer different questions. A partner who conflates them oversells, so both numbers appear on every search row.

One payment component, again

PTR-012 is WEB-012, GST-009 and POS-005 with charge-to-account added. Four platforms, one component, seven terminal states.
