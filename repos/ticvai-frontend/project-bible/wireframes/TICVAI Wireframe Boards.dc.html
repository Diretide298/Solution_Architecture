Every screen cites its source

Field names, states and deny reasons are read off the contracts in the package — access.yaml, orders, shift, finance, tenancy — and the mono line under each frame names the operation it came from.

The states, not just the screen

A payment has seven terminal outcomes and the one that matters is unknown. A scanner has eighteen deny reasons. Those are drawn, because a static happy path hides them.

What is still missing is marked

Stubs, workshop-blocked domains and rules nobody has written carry a flag. 136 screens had no definition at all; those boards say so in the header.

Where to start
Settle one system, three themes

Light guest, dark staff, light POS. That decision determines whether design-tokens is one package or three, and it blocks component extraction on every board.

Six components carry a third of it

Payment, the denomination counter, the scoped settings form, the pipeline stepper, the identity field group and the consent block. Each appears on three or four platforms.

Definition still precedes design

136 of these screens had no purpose, no operations and no states before this set. They are drawn from the contracts and marked as derived — they are a proposal to specify against, not a spec.
